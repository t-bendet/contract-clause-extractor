RING-BEARER ESCORT AND PROTECTION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Ring-Bearer Escort and Protection Services Agreement (the "Agreement")
is entered into as of the 25th day of December, Third Age 3018 (the
"Effective Date") by and between the Fellowship of the Ring, an unincorporated
association formed at the Council of Elrond, Rivendell (the "Fellowship"),
and Boromir, son of Denethor II, Steward-Prince of Gondor, residing at the
White Tower, Minas Tirith ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES. Contractor shall provide escort, protection, and general
questing services (the "Services") to the Ring-Bearer, one Frodo Baggins of
the Shire, in connection with the transport of one (1) Ring of Power (the
"Deliverable") to the Cracks of Doom, Orodruin, Mordor, for permanent
destruction and decommissioning.

2. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue
until the Deliverable is destroyed, or until Contractor's death, whichever
occurs first. There shall be no early termination for convenience. The
parties acknowledge that the expected duration of the engagement is unknown
and potentially without end.

3. COMPENSATION. Contractor shall receive no monetary compensation. All
Services are rendered in consideration of the continued existence of the
free peoples of Middle-earth, the value of which the parties agree is
incalculable and therefore unenforceable.

4. HANDLING OF THE DELIVERABLE. Contractor shall not touch, wear, carry,
borrow, request, demand, or attempt to seize the Deliverable under any
circumstances, including but not limited to circumstances in which
Contractor sincerely believes the Deliverable would be a gift, that it is
a strange fate that we should suffer so much fear and doubt over so small
a thing, or that it could be turned against the Enemy. Any breach of this
Section 4 shall be deemed material and incurable, and may result in
immediate removal from the Fellowship and/or arrow-related liability.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Contractor shall not disclose the existence, nature,
route, or destination of the Deliverable to any third party, including
family members, the Steward of Gondor, councils of war, or anyone in Osgiliath.
This confidentiality obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement
in perpetuity, throughout all Ages of Middle-earth.

6. LIABILITY. Contractor assumes all risk of injury, death, corruption by
dark forces, orc attack, cave troll encounter, Balrog proximity, and
madness induced by the Deliverable. The Fellowship makes no warranties,
express or implied, regarding Contractor's survival, and expressly
disclaims all liability for consequential damages, including the fall of
Gondor. Contractor's liability for breach of Section 4 shall be unlimited.

7. NON-COMPETE. During the term of this Agreement and for one hundred (100)
years thereafter, Contractor shall not provide escort, protection, or
artifact-transport services to any competing dark lord, wizard of many
colours, or other enterprise seeking dominion over Middle-earth.

8. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. In the event the Deliverable is not destroyed within
the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive
Ages unless the Council of Elrond provides written notice of non-renewal
delivered by Eagle no fewer than ninety (90) days before the end of the
then-current Age.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Gondor,
save that matters concerning the Deliverable shall be governed by the laws
of whoever currently holds it, which the parties acknowledge is a red flag.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF:

THE FELLOWSHIP OF THE RING          CONTRACTOR
By: Gandalf the Grey,               Boromir, son of Denethor,
    Authorized Signatory                Captain of the White Tower
