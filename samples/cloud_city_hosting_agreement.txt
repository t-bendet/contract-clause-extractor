CLOUD CITY HOSTING AND NON-INTERFERENCE AGREEMENT
(AS UNILATERALLY ALTERED)

This Hosting and Non-Interference Agreement (the "Agreement," or as
Provider prefers, the "Deal") is entered into as of the 4th day of the
month of Empire Day, 3 ABY (the "Effective Date") by and between the
Galactic Empire, acting through Lord Darth Vader, Dark Lord of the Sith
("Provider"), and Lando Calrissian, Baron Administrator of Cloud City,
Bespin ("Customer").

1. SERVICES. Provider agrees to permit continued operation of Cloud City
as an independent tibanna gas mining facility, free of Imperial garrison
or interference (the "Services"), subject to the amendment rights set
forth in Section 6, which Customer is strongly advised to read.

2. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS. In exchange for the Services, Customer shall:
(a) facilitate the arrival and detention of one Han Solo and associated
parties (the "Guests"); (b) provide dining facilities for one (1)
ambush-style dinner event; (c) surrender the Guests to Provider or, at
Provider's election, to a licensed third-party bounty subcontractor
(Boba Fett); and (d) refrain from warning anyone about anything.

3. TERM. This Agreement continues until Provider no longer finds it
useful. Customer has no termination rights. Provider may terminate at any
time, for any reason, or for no reason, with or without Force-choke.

4. SERVICE LEVELS. Provider makes no commitments regarding uptime,
freedom, or the continued liberty of Cloud City's residents. The sole
remedy for any service failure is Customer's ability to pray Provider
does not alter the Deal any further.

5. PAYMENT. No monetary payment is due from either party. Customer's
consideration consists of betrayed friendships and long-term reputational
damage. Provider's consideration consists of not garrisoning Cloud City
immediately, a promise Provider intends to keep only until Section 6 is
invoked.

6. UNILATERAL AMENDMENT. Provider may alter the Deal at any time, in any
respect, without notice, consent, or consideration. Amendments become
effective upon utterance. Customer's exclusive remedy is set forth in
Section 4. The parties acknowledge Provider's standard amendment notice
reads, in its entirety: "I am altering the deal. Pray I don't alter it
any further."

7. DATA AND PRIVACY. Provider may carbon-freeze any Guest for data
retention purposes. Frozen Guests shall be deemed adequately preserved
for shipping to third-party collectors (Jabba Desilijic Tiure) without
further consent. Hibernation sickness is expressly disclaimed.

8. LIABILITY. Provider's aggregate liability under this Agreement is
zero credits. Customer's liability is unlimited and may extend to
administrative staff, Wookiees, and protocol droids, whether intact or
in pieces.

9. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Customer shall not describe Provider's conduct as
a "betrayal," a "trap," or "getting worse all the time," notwithstanding
factual accuracy. This obligation survives termination for twenty (20)
standard years.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by Imperial law as
interpreted by Provider in his sole discretion. Venue for all disputes
lies exclusively aboard Executor, Provider's Super Star Destroyer, where
Customer is reminded that the Emperor is not as forgiving as Provider is.
