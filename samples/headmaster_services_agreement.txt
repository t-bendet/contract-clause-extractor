CONFIDENTIAL MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
(WITH UNBREAKABLE PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE)

This Confidential Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of
the 1st day of August, 1996 (the "Effective Date") by and between Albus
Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore, Headmaster, of Hogwarts School of
Witchcraft and Wizardry, Scotland ("Client"), and Severus Snape, Potions
Master and Head of Slytherin House, of the same address ("Provider").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES. Provider shall render the following services
(collectively, the "Services"): (a) double-agent intelligence services with
respect to a certain Dark Lord (the "Competitor"); (b) protective oversight
of one Draco Malfoy, a minor; (c) instruction in Defence Against the Dark
Arts, a position Provider acknowledges carries a documented one (1) year
average tenure; and (d) one (1) mercy killing of Client, upon Client's
verbal request, at a time and location of tactical convenience (the
"Final Deliverable").

2. TERM AND TERMINATION. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and
terminates upon completion of the Final Deliverable. Provider may not
terminate this Agreement for any reason, including conscience, reputational
harm, or being widely despised by colleagues and students. Client may
terminate at will, or by dying, whichever comes first.

3. COMPENSATION. Provider shall receive no additional compensation beyond
his existing teaching salary, the parties agreeing that absolution,
redemption, and the memory of Lily Potter constitute adequate and
irrevocable consideration. Provider waives any right to renegotiate.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Provider shall maintain absolute secrecy regarding the
Services, the Final Deliverable, Client's cursed hand, the existence of
certain soul-fragment containers, and all related plans, even where
disclosure would materially improve Provider's personal safety, public
reputation, or posthumous portrait placement. This obligation survives the
death of either party indefinitely and binds Provider's memories, whether
kept in his head or extracted into a Pensieve.

5. PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE. The parties acknowledge that portions of the
Services are secured by an Unbreakable Vow executed with a third party
(Narcissa Malfoy) on or about the Effective Date. Provider acknowledges
that failure to perform shall result in immediate death, which the parties
agree constitutes liquidated damages and not a penalty.

6. INDEMNIFICATION. Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Client
against all claims arising from the Final Deliverable, including claims by
the Ministry of Magic, the Order of the Phoenix, and public opinion.
Client provides no reciprocal indemnity. Provider acknowledges he will be
blamed for everything and agrees not to seek contribution.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. All spells, potions improvements, and marginalia
authored by Provider, whether before or during the term, including works
signed as the Half-Blood Prince, are hereby assigned to Client in
perpetuity, without attribution or royalty.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION. This Agreement is governed by
Wizarding Law of Great Britain. Disputes shall be resolved exclusively by
Client, whose decisions are final, binding, and typically explained only
years later, after it is too late to object.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties execute this Agreement in invisible ink.
