SENIOR PARTNER COEXISTENCE AND NON-AGGRESSION AGREEMENT

This Senior Partner Coexistence and Non-Aggression Agreement (the
"Agreement") is entered into as of the 15th day of June, 2013 (the
"Effective Date") by and between Harvey Reginald Specter, Senior Partner
("Specter"), and Louis Marlowe Litt, Senior Partner ("Litt"), each of
Pearson Hardman LLP, 601 Fifth Avenue, New York, New York (the "Firm"),
and witnessed under protest by Jessica Pearson, Managing Partner.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Specter is, by his own repeated assertion, the best closer in
New York City; and

WHEREAS, Litt objects to the foregoing recital and demands its inclusion
be noted as contested; and

WHEREAS, the parties' ongoing hostilities have resulted in poached
associates, hijacked clients, mud-throwing (literal), and the misuse of
the Firm's ballet tickets;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. NON-AGGRESSION. Neither party shall: (a) poach, reassign, or emotionally
destabilize the other party's associates, including but not limited to
first-year associates with questionable academic credentials; (b) contact
the other party's clients without prior written consent, a courtesy the
parties acknowledge neither has ever extended; or (c) deploy Donna Paulsen
as an intelligence asset against the other party, the parties acknowledging
that Donna already knows everything anyway.

2. ASSOCIATE SHARING. Litt shall be entitled to supervise Firm associates
for training purposes no more than two (2) days per week. Specter shall be
entitled to take credit for the results. The parties agree this allocation
reflects existing practice.

3. CLIENT OWNERSHIP. Clients originated by a party remain that party's
clients in perpetuity, regardless of merit, effort, or which party
actually performs the work. Billable credit shall follow ownership and
not labor, which the associates may discover at their own pace.

4. COMPENSATION AND BONUSES. Nothing in this Agreement modifies either
party's compensation. Litt's request for a compensation-parity clause was
heard, considered, and denied in the time it took Specter to say no.

5. DICTAPHONE PRIVACY. Specter shall not access, quote from, or perform
dramatic readings of Litt's dictaphone recordings, regardless of comedic
value. Litt acknowledges that recordings already in circulation cannot be
recalled and constitute a pre-existing condition.

6. MUDDING. The parties' shared mudding appointments, if any, shall be
scheduled through Donna and are subject to a strict confidentiality
obligation surviving termination of this Agreement for ninety-nine (99)
years. Neither party shall acknowledge the other's presence at the spa.

7. TERM AND RENEWAL. This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year
and shall renew automatically for successive one-year terms unless either
party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days
prior to renewal, delivered by hand, by Donna, or by being left
passive-aggressively on the other party's chair.

8. BREACH AND REMEDIES. In the event of breach, the non-breaching party's
remedies shall be limited to: (a) an apology, sincerity not warranted;
(b) one (1) public acknowledgment that the breaching party is "a great
lawyer"; or (c) binding arbitration before Jessica Pearson, whose ruling
is final and whose disappointment is a penalty the parties agree exceeds
any monetary damages. Neither party shall be liable for consequential
damages, hurt feelings, or ruined ballet evenings.

9. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Neither party shall disparage the other to clients,
associates, or the press. Internal disparagement within the Firm's
hallways, elevators, and file rooms is expressly permitted and, the
parties acknowledge, inevitable. Litt shall not be referred to as "the
new Harvey" ironically. Specter shall not be referred to as "irreplaceable"
in Litt's presence.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
New York. The parties waive jury trial, the parties agreeing that no jury
of ordinary citizens could be expected to tolerate either of them for the
duration of a trial.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement in
triplicate, one copy of which Litt has already had framed.
